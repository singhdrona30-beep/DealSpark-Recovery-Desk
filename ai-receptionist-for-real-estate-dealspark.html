AI Receptionist for Real Estate Businesses

Real estate agents may be driving, showing a property or meeting clients when an inquiry arrives. A structured intake workflow can capture who called, which property they are asking about and when they prefer a callback—without implying that an agent has confirmed a showing.

Capture the details agents need

  • Record the caller’s name and preferred contact method.
  • Capture the property address, listing reference or general buying/selling question.
  • Separate buyer inquiries, seller leads, rental questions and existing-client requests.
  • Collect a preferred showing window as a request, then leave confirmation to the agent.
  • Route time-sensitive or private matters to the responsible agent or brokerage team.

Keep the handoff clear

Set up a real-estate receptionist with approved answers about business hours and the next step—not invented listing details, availability, pricing or legal advice. The workflow should clearly tell callers when a human must confirm a showing, verify a property fact or respond to a transaction-specific question.

How to evaluate the workflow

Start with the calls your team receives most often. Write down the approved questions, the details staff need for a useful callback, and the situations that must go directly to a person. Test the sample workflow before connecting a live phone line or customer system.

DealSpark’s public demos use sample data. Live phone routing, calendar access and external integrations require separate configuration, authorization and testing.
