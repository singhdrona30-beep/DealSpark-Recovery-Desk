Customer Terms & Fair-Use Policy

These commercial terms are designed to make DealSpark pricing predictable while protecting customers and DealSpark from unexpected third-party usage costs.

1. Included usage

Starter — $79/month: up to 300 website AI conversations per month.

Growth — $149/month: up to 750 website AI conversations and 300 AI phone minutes per month.

Pro — $249/month: up to 1,500 website AI conversations and 750 AI phone minutes per month.

Business — $399/month: up to 3,000 website AI conversations and 1,500 AI phone minutes per month.

Unused monthly allowances do not roll over. Individual product purchases use the allowance stated in the order or subscription confirmation.

2. Overage and service protection

Usage above the included allowance may be subject to an agreed overage rate or service pause. DealSpark will disclose the applicable overage rate before charging an overage. DealSpark will not silently move a customer to a higher recurring subscription.

3. Third-party charges

Telephony, carrier, SMS, email delivery, AI-model, payment-processing and other third-party charges are separate unless expressly included in the customer's order. Third-party pricing can change independently of DealSpark.

4. Fair use

DealSpark is intended for genuine business customer interactions. Spam, automated abuse, traffic flooding, artificial call generation, illegal activity, or activity that creates disproportionate third-party costs is not permitted. DealSpark may suspend abusive or unsafe usage.

5. Launch discount

The new-customer launch offer is 10% off the first three monthly subscription charges after completing a DealSpark demo. It does not discount usage-based or third-party charges and cannot be combined with another discount unless DealSpark agrees in writing.

6. Changes and upgrades

DealSpark may recommend a higher plan when usage regularly approaches or exceeds the included allowance. Customers will be informed of the recommended plan and price before a recurring upgrade.

7. Free demos

Free demos are demonstrations of DealSpark functionality and are not a promise of unlimited production usage. Production usage is governed by the customer's selected plan.

8. Agreement

The customer's order, subscription confirmation and any signed service agreement control if they contain terms that differ from this general website policy.

This page is commercial website language, not legal advice. Have final customer agreements reviewed by qualified counsel for the jurisdictions in which DealSpark operates.
