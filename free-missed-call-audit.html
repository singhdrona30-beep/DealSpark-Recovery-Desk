What to include in the review

  • Where calls go when nobody can answer.
  • How caller details and service requests are recorded.
  • Who owns the next callback and how overdue follow-ups are spotted.
  • Which requests need a human response or urgent escalation.
  • Whether an answering service, an improved team process, or automation is worth evaluating.

Three questions to start with

  1. How many calls or inquiries do you think go unanswered in a typical week?
  2. What usually happens after a missed call?
  3. How do you know whether the inquiry became a conversation, appointment, or sale?

You do not need to send customer names, phone numbers, medical information, or other sensitive details. General workflow information is enough.
